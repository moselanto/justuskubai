About the book

Every name tells a story. Make yours one worth remembering.

A good name is rather to be chosen than great riches, and loving favour rather than silver and gold.

Proverbs 22:1

In The Memory of a Name, you are invited to reflect on the importance of character, relationships, kindness, and the actions that leave lasting impressions on the hearts of others.

Drawing from biblical wisdom and practical life lessons, this book explores how our choices shape the memories people carry of us long after we have left their presence.

Whether in our families, friendships, churches, workplaces, or communities, we all have an opportunity to make a difference. The question is: what will people remember when our names come up?

This book will encourage you to value relationships, live with integrity, refresh the hearts of others, and build a legacy that honours God.

Your name is being remembered in someone's story. Make that memory count.

About the author

Pastor Justus Kubai

Founder & Lead Pastor, DC Kabete N MarketFounder & Executive Director, Cistern of Hope FoundationMarriage Therapist & Counselor

Justus M. Kubai is a Kenyan pastor, marriage therapist, counselor and community leader, known for his impact in both spiritual and community development. He gave his life to Christ in the early 2000s and was discipled at Deliverance Church Kangemi, later serving as an associate pastor for seven years before planting Deliverance Church Kabete N in Uthiru, Nairobi, in 2017.

He holds a Diploma in Theology from KAG East University and international certifications in mission work, leadership and church planting, and is a Certified Trainer with Dynamic Church Planting International. Through the Cistern of Hope Foundation he leads work that uplifts marginalized communities in Kenya through skills training, youth counseling and small-business programmes.

As a counselor, he blends biblical principles with practical strategies to help couples and families build lasting, healthy relationships. He is married to Pastor Clare Auma, and together they are blessed with four children.
